Rectangular Plate

A wide plate with your number and a glowing pattern

A wide rectangular plate with a large glowing number on the left and a cut-out pattern along the side and bottom that lets warm light through, with a soft halo on the wall. Suits pillars and wide walls.

You can choose

  • House number
  • Size
  • Finish: Black, Gray, Dark brown
  • Font
  • Warm light (2700–3000K)

Mounting: Wall mounted

This plate always stays wide. Want a tall plate? Choose “Vertical Illuminated Plate”. BULB13 confirms the final size with you.
